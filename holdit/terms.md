## 2. Safety rules you must follow
- Use the App **only on dry land, seated or lying down**. Never use it in, under or near water.
- **Never practise breath-holding in water alone.** Shallow-water blackout can occur without warning and can cause drowning, even in strong and experienced swimmers.
- Do not hyperventilate before breath-holds.
- Never use the App while driving, operating machinery, bathing, standing, or in any situation where loss of consciousness could cause injury.
- Consult a qualified physician before use if you have, or suspect you have, any heart, lung, blood-pressure, seizure, or other medical condition, or if you are pregnant.
- Stop immediately if you feel dizzy, light-headed, tingling, confused, or experience vision changes or any discomfort.
