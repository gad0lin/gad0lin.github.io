# HoldIt: Apnea Tables — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

HoldIt does **not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data**.

- Your settings, training sessions, max-hold results, XP and trophies are stored **only on your device**, inside the app's private storage.
- The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs, and it makes no network requests.
- Deleting the app deletes all of its data. You can also clear your history at any time in Settings → Clear history.

If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships.

Contact: use the support link on the App Store page (https://github.com/gad0lin/gad0lin.github.io/issues).
